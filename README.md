# 📊 Konovo Command Center Dashboard

> A comprehensive analytics and performance monitoring suite built for the IRIS / Apollo Intelligence platform — an enterprise healthcare research SaaS.

---

## 🎯 What This Is

A **master dashboard portal** with **15+ individual team dashboards** built to give VP-level and engineering leadership real-time visibility into ticket trends, team performance, and closure rates across the entire IRIS platform.

Built entirely from scratch using **HTML, CSS, Chart.js, and embedded JSON** — no backend required.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo

👉 **[View Live Dashboard](https://vidyachoki.github.io/konovo-command-center-dashboard/)**

---

## 📋 What It Tracks

- Ticket distribution across **10+ dev teams** (Neptune, Pluto, Mercury, AIM, NEXUS, NOVA, IGNITE, QUAL, LITE, SPARQ, and more)
- Team-wise **closure rates and SLA performance**
- **Weekly and monthly trends** for ticket volume
- **P1/P2/P3/P4 priority breakdown** per team
- Cross-team comparison and bottleneck identification
- Individual team drill-down dashboards

## 💡 Why I Built This

As a Production Support Engineer supporting 10+ dev teams, I needed a way to:
- Present ticket trend data to VP and engineering managers in weekly meetings
- Identify which teams had the highest backlog and needed escalation
- Track SLA performance across teams over time

This dashboard replaced manual Excel reports and saved **hours of weekly reporting time**.
